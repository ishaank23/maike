Privacy

What we keep, and why

When you leave a number or book a call, we keep your name, phone number, the area you live in, your baby’s due date or date of birth, the languages and tradition you chose, and the plan you asked about. We use it to call you, to message you on WhatsApp or SMS about Maike, and to match you to a carer or a care team.

Your baby’s care file (logs, notes, weights, prescriptions you photograph) is health information. It is seen only by the people on your team and the nurse who reads the log. It is never sold or shared with advertisers, hospitals or insurers. You can ask for a copy or ask us to delete it at any time by writing to hello@maike.in.

Carers are matched on the languages and tradition you choose. We never record or match on anyone’s caste, religion or place of origin.

You can stop WhatsApp and SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.
